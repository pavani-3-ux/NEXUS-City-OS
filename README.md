NEXUS City OS is an AI-powered Smart City Intelligence &amp; Operations Platform that unifies real-time data from traffic, air quality, energy, water, public transport, weather, emergency services, and citizen systems. It delivers interactive analytics, predictive insights, and KPI dashboards to help city leaders make faster, data-driven decisions.


# 🌆 NEXUS City OS

### AI-Powered Smart City Intelligence & Operations Platform

NEXUS City OS is a next-generation Smart City Analytics platform designed to centralize, monitor, and analyze urban data from multiple city systems. The platform combines real-time and historical data from traffic, air quality, energy, water, weather, public transportation, emergency services, and citizen feedback to provide actionable insights for city administrators and decision-makers.

By integrating advanced analytics, interactive dashboards, and predictive intelligence, NEXUS City OS enables smarter urban planning, operational efficiency, sustainability monitoring, and improved citizen experiences.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Modern cities generate massive amounts of data every day. However, this data often exists in isolated systems, making it difficult to gain a unified view of city operations.

NEXUS City OS solves this challenge by creating a centralized analytics platform that transforms raw urban data into meaningful insights through visualization, forecasting, and performance monitoring.
